# Built for a small screen

The XZ1 Compact has a 4.6-inch screen of 720 × 1280 pixels. Lilac's interface was rebuilt for it, so the camera, the gallery and the editor work with one thumb and every word fits.

How to use each one: [Getting started](getting-started.md#the-camera-screen). What makes it work on a small screen:

- **No top bar.** The picture starts at the top edge. The top line shows only settings you changed, so it is usually empty.
- **Everything you press is at the bottom**, within reach of the thumb that holds the phone.
- **The mode dial is the shutter.** Swipe it sideways; the faint marks beside it are the next modes.
- **The zoom control sits just above the shutter**, in reach of the thumb: a thumbwheel by default, or a pill or a curved collar like the zoom lever round a compact camera's shutter button (Settings › Setup › Interface).
- **The picture takes the gestures** for the rest: down for the everyday row, up for manual controls, sideways for looks.
- **One sheet for quick settings**, rising from the bottom. Tap a setting's name for a one-line explanation.
- **The side buttons are camera controls,** as on a compact camera, so you can shoot without reaching across the screen. See [Buttons on the side](getting-started.md#buttons-on-the-side).
- **Short words.** Labels and messages fit in one or two lines; short notes appear for a moment in a small pill above the shutter.

## The editor

The photo editor follows the same rules:

- **Sections along the bottom,** in thumb reach, like the camera's controls.
- **Slim rulers** you drag with one finger, on the photo's bottom edge.
- **The icons along the top fade** while your finger is on the photo or a ruler, so nothing covers what you are changing.
- **Compare by holding:** hold to see the original, let go to return, so comparing needs no extra screen.
- **Fast preview** from small copies, so editing stays quick on a 4 GB phone.

The editor's controls, one by one: [Editor](editor.md).
