# Optimizations and tweaks

Lilac started as a fork of [PhotonCamera](https://github.com/bjzhou/PhotonCamera) in early November 2026. This page lists what Lilac has changed on its own since then, in three groups: Sony's camera features, GCam's multi-frame processing and the memory work behind it, and the rest of the app. It does not list PhotonCamera's own features.

Each entry says what changed and how, and ends with a link to the exact change on GitHub for anyone who wants the technical detail. The measurements were taken on an Xperia XZ1 Compact, a phone with 4 GB of memory and a 4.6-inch screen. Most of the work follows from those two facts, and from the aim of bringing Sony's camera features and GCam's processing to this phone.

??? info "Words used on this page"

    RAW
    :   The sensor's unprocessed picture data. It holds more detail than a JPEG and is developed afterwards.

    Stacking (merging)
    :   Taking several shots and combining them into one photo with less noise and more detail.

    Burst
    :   Several shots in quick succession. Lilac merges the shots of a burst into one photo.

    HDR
    :   Combining a darker and a brighter shot so that both the bright and the dark parts of a scene keep their detail.

    Noise
    :   The grainy speckle that shows up in dim photos.

    ISO
    :   The camera's light sensitivity. A higher ISO brightens a dim scene and adds more noise.

    Exposure
    :   How much light a photo gets, set by the shutter speed and the ISO.

    Viewfinder
    :   The live picture on the screen while you frame a shot.

    Frame
    :   One picture of a burst or a video.

    GPU
    :   The phone's graphics chip. Lilac uses it for much of the photo processing, and on this phone it has to share its memory with everything else.

    Look (LUT)
    :   A colour style applied to a photo, such as a film look.

    Bokeh
    :   The soft blur behind a subject in a portrait.

    MP
    :   Megapixels, the size of a picture. The phone's back camera takes 19 MP photos.

## Sony camera features

Lilac's work on Sony's own camera features: face and subject tracking, focus, Sony's photo processing and slow motion. What is done and what is not yet is on [Sony camera features](hardware.md).

### Focus, faces and exposure

- **Sony-style face frames and subject tracking.** Up to five faces get a light frame that glides smoothly, and tapping something that is not a face follows that object. Focus and exposure are steered to the tracked face or object, using the phone's own face detection and tracking, as Sony's camera does. ([`d115118`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/d1151185))
- **Half-press on the camera key holds focus and exposure, like a Sony camera.** Half-pressing locks focus and exposure on your tapped point or the centre; a point already in focus shows green at once with a light tick, and a miss is focused again. An amber exposure lock mark shows on the top edge. ([`f9a19ab`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f9a19ab8), [`a5050a8`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/a5050a8e))
- **Smile shutter.** A new Smile tile takes the photo by itself when the main face smiles as much as you set (small, average or big), at most once a second. A gauge on the face frame fills with the smile. Smile and the self-timer switch each other off. ([`3bd1e2f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3bd1e2f5))
- **Faces are found with the phone held sideways.** Eye focus and Portrait no longer lose the face in landscape, on the back and front camera. The viewfinder picture is turned to match the phone's rotation before faces are looked for. ([`79611ed`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/79611edb), [`03c9339`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/03c93396))
- **A Sony-style focus frame.** A tap draws a thin square: white while focusing, green with a light tick when focus locks, three white blinks on a miss. On the front camera, which cannot report focus, a tap now only sets exposure and shows an exposure circle. ([`c145afa`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/c145afa3))
- **Metering circle like Sony's.** In Spot metering (measuring brightness at one point) a small circle sits in the centre and moves with your tap, and can be dragged elsewhere. Centre-weighted metering shows it fixed in the centre; other modes show none. Starting tracking no longer sends it to the centre first. ([`f9a19ab`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f9a19ab8), [`28b3a75`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/28b3a751), [`d9ae10c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/d9ae10c9))
- **A tapped focus point stays put in dim light.** Leftover results from the previous focus lock and ongoing brightness changes were mistaken for a scene change, so the point was dropped within about a second. The app now waits for the real focus result and for exposure to settle. ([`2b19e20`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/2b19e202), [`60b812d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/60b812d2))
- **Bigger focus area in dim light.** From ISO 1600 up, a small focus area is enlarged, because a tiny dark patch holds too little detail to focus on. In a test, a half-press on a far background went from missing to focusing in about 1.5 s. ([`825e5db`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/825e5dbd))
- **Subject focus searches are no longer cut short.** A search used to be ended early by a leftover "focused" result from before it started, so many were cancelled almost at once. They now wait for their own result, and the time limit grows from 2 s to 4 s so the lens can finish a full sweep. ([`71576ff`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/71576ff3))
- **No endless focus hunting in the dark.** In a scene too dark to focus, the lens used to sweep its whole range again every 2 s. After two misses in a row, it now stops searching until the subject changes, you tap or choose a face, or continuous focus finds focus again. ([`bd2c75b`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/bd2c75bf))
- **Far subjects the camera cannot focus on get a sensible fallback.** If a tap, half-press or subject search ends unfocused and the back camera's laser distance sensor (which sees only to about 2 m) sees nothing, the lens is set so everything from about 2 m outwards is sharp. Automatic focus returns on the next tap or scene change. ([`4c82b7e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4c82b7e1))
- **A tap on a plain area focuses on that spot.** A blank or dark area has no object to follow, and the tracking box used to wander off. If it drifts or is lost soon after the tap, tracking stops and the tap becomes an ordinary fixed focus point. ([`3824610`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3824610d))
- **Taps on the front camera land in the right place.** The front camera's picture is mirrored, and the app undid the mirroring in the wrong order, so on most front cameras a tap was measured at the opposite point of the picture. It now maps correctly. ([`f30a417`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f30a4174))
- **An old tapped point clears itself.** A tapped point is dropped about 10 seconds after its mark fades, so a later half-press starts from the centre again. A point whose brightness you moved away from the focus mark stays until your next tap. ([`a5050a8`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/a5050a8e))

### Front camera

- **Choose Fast or Accurate colour on the front camera.** In dim light, front camera photos come out violet-magenta with lifted blacks. A new quick settings choice, Accurate, gives true colours by using Sony's own photo processing, at about 4 frames per second, so bursts are slower; Fast keeps 30. The live view is also adjusted to look closer to the photo. ([`3ca889d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3ca889d0), [`4e5bfbb`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4e5bfbb1), [`611065c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/611065c0), [`8853928`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/8853928c), [`9bde3a4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/9bde3a42), [`e397c24`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/e397c24f))
- **The screen lights your face as a flash.** The front camera has no flash, so Flash on now turns the screen white and its backlight to full for the shot. The white stays until the whole capture, merged shots included, has ended. It works in Photo and Portrait. ([`767760f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/767760f1))
- **The front camera starts at 2 MP, with its own size setting.** The 2 MP size gives cleaner photos in dim light, while larger front sizes come out with crushed black shadows and garish colours at high sensitivity. Full size is one tap away, and a size you chose earlier is kept. ([`11794c9`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/11794c95), [`fa73d5e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/fa73d5e3))
- **Night always uses 2 MP on the front camera.** Whatever size Photo mode has chosen, Night uses 2 MP, and your choice comes back when you leave Night. Quick settings show it as a fixed 2 MP. ([`ccc58a4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/ccc58a44))
- **No more brightness flicker at full size.** On the front camera in Photo mode at full size, the live view's brightness no longer swings several times a second. The app sends one small focus nudge just after the preview starts and puts focus back after about a second. ([`104c2af`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/104c2af3), [`3b36edd`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3b36edd2))
- **Switching to a camera without manual exposure no longer freezes the readings.** After going from a camera with manual ISO and shutter to one without, such as the front camera, the other camera froze its readings and greyed out exposure adjustment until restart. Such a camera now always meters automatically. ([`68999a6`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/68999a61))

### Video and slow motion

- **960 frames per second slow motion.** On the Xperia XZ1 Compact the app can now record Sony's super slow motion. Sony's photo service only talks to a privileged system app, so a small companion app does the recording and Lilac talks to it. Only Lilac may use it. ([`20ddd3e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/20ddd3e5), [`d2f30f4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/d2f30f4e))
- **60 fps at 1080p and 120 fps at 720p.** With the Lilac Sony Bridge, Video offers two more frame rates through Sony's own video mode. Lilac draws the frames itself, so looks, sound and Pause work as in normal video, and 120 fps files carry the rate so other apps read them correctly. ([`b4cae10`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b4cae104), [`ffe5eca`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/ffe5ecaf), [`0683bea`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/0683beae), [`2b7e64d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/2b7e64d7))
- **Sony's video stabilisers.** Stabilise now steps through Off, EIS+, Steady (SteadyShot) and Active (Intelligent Active), offering each only at the sizes and rates where it works. ([`adccee5`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/adccee53))
- **The gallery plays 120 fps videos slowed.** A 120 fps video opens four times slower, with a switch to full speed, and Share and Export ask Slowed or Full speed. ([`1101088`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/11010885), [`585f630`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/585f6305))
- **Slow-mo switch and burst button.** In Video mode a Slow-mo item in quick settings (shown only where available) turns it on for the next recording, at 720p with 177-frame bursts. While recording, the camera key or a footer icon fires the burst. Choosing another resolution turns it off with a note, and torch and stabilisation are greyed out. ([`75da159`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/75da1591), [`b396ecb`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b396ecb9), [`1210c48`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/1210c486), [`1062d11`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/1062d113), [`4d29705`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4d297053))
- **Slow-motion videos have continuous sound.** The microphone is recorded separately and laid onto the video afterwards, silent over the slow part. Gaps are filled with silence, because the gallery's player used to stall where the burst starts. ([`d2f30f4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/d2f30f4e), [`5ecf21d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/5ecf21de))
- **Progress and a warning for slow motion.** While the phone reads out a burst, a pill counts the frames as they arrive and shows a check mark when all are in. A "Too dark for slow-mo" pill appears when the shot would need an ISO above 1600. ([`1062d11`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/1062d113), [`75da159`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/75da1591))
- **Recording stops and saves before the phone overheats.** Android does not report heat on this phone, so a recording could run until the camera was killed and the file lost. The app now reads the camera sensor, battery and board temperatures itself, shows a warming gauge, and stops with a dialog saying what stopped it and how far to cool. ([`9b75686`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/9b756866), [`53aaff0`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/53aaff04), [`b34b6df`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b34b6df3))
- **Pause and Resume keep the picture in video.** After resuming, the file had sound but the picture stopped at the first pause, because frames arriving during the pause blocked the camera. Those frames are now discarded, so the picture continues. ([`bf725f4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/bf725f49))
- **The recording time sits in a centred pill.** While recording, the time shows in a pill at the centre of the top line (amber or red when the phone is hot), with the remaining recording time beside it once under 20 minutes of storage are left. Paused, a pause mark replaces the blinking dot. ([`2300928`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/23009289))


## GCam processing and memory

Merging frames the way GCam does, and making it fit in 4 GB of memory.

### Photo and Night

- **Night mode on the dial.** Night is now a mode of its own. Every shot merges 6 to 12 frames on Auto, planned for low light after the phone measures how much it shakes. A Fast or Accurate switch and a "Hold still" message guide you. ([`6af567e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6af567e4))
- **Night suggestion in dim light.** The phone's light sensor reading shows on the top line when it is dark. After two seconds of dim light, Photo mode offers a button that switches to Night. ([`6af567e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6af567e4))
- **Merged photos start from the steadiest shot.** The app uses the phone's motion sensor to find the steadiest of the first four shots and lines the others up on it. This also works on the front camera and when some shots have no motion data. ([`414297c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/414297c4), [`e5740b3`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/e5740b36), [`b431298`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b431298d), [`f8267ed`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f8267edd), [`3132984`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/31329845))
- **Less colour noise in merged dim-light photos.** The merge used to mistake ordinary noise for movement and barely used most frames. It now measures each frame's real noise first. At ISO 5120 it uses 7.2 frames' worth of 12 instead of 1.7, and colour noise is halved. ([`4debfa1`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4debfa12), [`aef51ae`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/aef51ae8), [`72d9dd1`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/72d9dd17))
- **Pink, hazy shadows and magenta tints removed in dim light.** Dim shots, worst on the front camera, had pink shadows and a magenta cast. Each photo is now measured and corrected, and dim ones get a gentle shadow lift. HDR and RAW photos are unchanged. ([`ad7ae19`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/ad7ae193), [`0cda30f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/0cda30f3), [`c2da80d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/c2da80d5))
- **Low-light photos are as bright as the preview.** The low-light plan aimed one stop darker than the normal preview and now matches it. The brightening of merged dark photos was also too weak (about 1.18 times instead of 1.44) and is now applied correctly. ([`ea83c42`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/ea83c429), [`7171cf4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7171cf42))
- **Darker bursts when the sky or lights would burn out.** When the viewfinder shows bright areas turned pure white, a RAW burst is shot 1 to 3 stops darker. The merge then brings the shadows back up, so those highlights stay recoverable. ([`5b125a7`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/5b125a70))
- **Merged bursts keep their natural brightness.** A merged burst used to be pulled back down to match the dark viewfinder, undoing the cleaner shadows. Several small mistakes were fixed, so the final photo keeps the brighter, better-exposed result. ([`7273c43`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7273c43e), [`f9e6c2a`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f9e6c2af), [`357f5e6`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/357f5e6d), [`b616857`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b6168576))
- **HDR shots adapt to the scene.** Photo mode's HDR used to take fixed darker and brighter shots. It now picks how much darker or brighter from the bright and near-black areas in the viewfinder, and uses a longer exposure on a steady phone so shadows are less noisy. ([`6dc3b22`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6dc3b227))
- **HDR shots fire the moment you press.** The app watches how steady the phone is while the viewfinder is open, so it no longer waits half a second. The tap on the shutter no longer counts as shake, so a tripod gets the longer, cleaner exposure. ([`199c8dd`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/199c8dd8), [`8901922`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/89019228))
- **A tripod no longer counts as shaky.** The motion sensor read a small steady offset, so the low-light plan always chose its shortest exposure. Only real hand tremor is counted now. ([`caf0538`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/caf0538e))
- **Multi-frame shots stay inside the camera's allowed ISO range.** When the chosen ISO was above what the camera accepts for manual settings, the request was refused. The value is now brought down into range with the same exposure time. ([`c396169`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/c3961698))

### Pro mode and RAW

- **Pro mode RAW works on the Xperia XZ1 Compact.** On the LineageOS build with the RAW add-on by sunplex07, a RAW-only request made the camera crash. The app now also asks for a full-size JPEG with each RAW shot and discards it, which fixes this. ([`f96b0f3`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f96b0f33))
- **Corners of RAW photos are no longer dark.** The rear camera gave no useful correction for corner darkening, so corners got about 0.23 of the centre's light. A correction measured on this phone is now applied and saved in the RAW file, bringing corners to 0.93 to 0.97. ([`6f36b8e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6f36b8e2))
- **RAW photos with noise reduction no longer come out black.** Any RAW photo with brightness or colour noise reduction turned on came out solid black. The cause was fixed, which also saves one full-size copy per photo. ([`11a5c1c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/11a5c1c3))
- **Pro photos keep their deep shadows.** Photos developed in the background came out with darker, crushed shadows than the same shot developed directly. Both now handle black the same way, and older RAW files from the app also develop correctly. ([`33e0f72`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/33e0f722))
- **Re-render a finished Pro photo as a copy.** Develop a Pro photo again with different settings, such as noise reduction, sharpening or exposure, into a new photo next to the untouched original. Start it from the photo's More sheet, or from several selected photos in the gallery. ([`2feac61`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/2feac61f), [`9742ad3`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/9742ad31))
- **A built-in noise profile measured on this phone.** The profile comes from a tripod test of 20 shots from ISO 40 to 2884. The phone's own profile is up to 3.7 times off, so the measured one is now the default and the system one is labelled as inaccurate here. ([`f9adbee`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f9adbee1), [`526df14`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/526df14b), [`add4dc5`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/add4dc53), [`0abca2a`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/0abca2a5))
- **The camera's JPEG in Pro mode matches the finished photo.** It used to be saved as the full sensor frame and could be turned or flipped differently. It is now cropped to the shot's shape and turned the same way. ([`8ad6f41`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/8ad6f419))
- **HDR ratio slider in Pro quick settings.** A slider from 1 to 20 limits how far the HDR merge may go. The photo info card also shows the limit and the ratio actually used. ([`0e6b1bd`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/0e6b1bdf))
- **Pro mode says when something is unavailable.** ISO and shutter controls are greyed out on cameras without manual exposure, such as the front camera. Tapping a greyed Pro button explains why, and names the RAW add-on by sunplex07 on this phone's back camera. ([`b3098e3`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b3098e3a), [`6d9e027`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6d9e0279))

### Memory and keeping the app alive

- **Pro photos are developed in the background, one at a time.** After a single-frame Pro shot, the RAW file is saved at once and developed later by a separate helper that exits after each photo and hands its graphics memory back. Swiping the app away from the recent apps screen does not stop it. Before, one 19 MP Pro photo could get the app killed by Android's low-memory killer. ([`8216df7`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/8216df71), [`eb43756`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/eb437565), [`8b41c8e`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/8b41c8e7), [`a2c7154`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/a2c7154c), [`3bac2cb`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3bac2cb1), [`3dfe567`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3dfe5678))
- **HDR merging fits in memory on 4 GB phones.** Merging three exposures at 19 MP used about 1.5 GB of GPU memory at its peak. The app now frees the temporary pictures as soon as they are merged, with the same result and a peak about a third lower. ([`1cadd81`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/1cadd811))
- **Big work areas are freed sooner.** The full-size RAW work areas (about 612 MB) are released at the end of each render. The helper also frees its full-size pictures before building the HDR versions, cutting its peak from 1.42 GB with identical output. ([`8bfff7c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/8bfff7cf), [`c5fecde`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/c5fecdef))
- **Renders wait for free memory and step aside for Pro shots.** Renders started with little free memory, or beside a Pro shot, were killed. The queue now waits up to 1 minute for 450 MB free (showing "Waiting for free memory") and for a Pro shot to be saved, and runs at lower priority so the render is stopped rather than the app. ([`064d9de`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/064d9de8), [`7ab20b1`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7ab20b1d), [`e11a8d8`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/e11a8d83))
- **A stopped render continues instead of starting over.** After the RAW development the app saves a checkpoint, so a retry skips the 20-25 s development and keeps the HDR versions. A photo that really fails 3 times is marked failed with its RAW file kept. The queue also restarts itself if Android kills it. ([`a9a6296`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/a9a62964), [`4d98e4b`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4d98e4b4), [`93609ed`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/93609ed7))
- **Background blur fits this phone's GPU.** The blur now works on a smaller copy here, and the editor's GPU jobs take turns with the preview first. Before, a blur started during a preview crashed the GPU and every later render failed until the app restarted. A test picture went from a timeout to 5 of 5 clean renders. ## The rest of the app

Speed, the camera screen, the editor, the gallery, reliability and housekeeping.

### Starting up and switching modes

Lilac opens its camera sooner and switches modes with fewer stalls. These times were measured on an Xperia XZ1 Compact with a release build (the kind you install), in seconds from tapping the icon, as the middle value of 4 to 7 cold starts. "Before" is Lilac as it was before this work.

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Camera connected | 1.02 | **0.40** |
| First picture, Photo mode | 1.78 | **1.41** |
| First picture, Pro mode | 1.59 | **1.25** |
| Video → Photo, tap to first picture | 1.45 (camera opened twice) | **0.99** |
| Photo → Pro, tap to first picture | 0.87 | 0.81 |

For comparison, Open Camera shows its first picture after 0.90 s and Aperture after 1.52 s on the same phone. Photo mode stays about 0.26 s slower than Pro because it asks the sensor for its full-size picture, which gives better stacked photos.

- **The camera starts before the screen is ready.** Lilac opens the camera as soon as its settings are read and sets up the photo session before the viewfinder exists, instead of waiting for the screen. - **Video → Photo opens the camera once, not twice.** The old switch rebuilt the viewfinder twice, so the camera opened twice, and the second time cost about half a second. ([`2afcb36`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/2afcb36a))
- **Changing mode no longer waits for the settings to be saved.** Switching between Photo and Pro asked the camera to reopen only after saving settings, which took 0.25 to 0.3 s. ### Reliability fixes

- **The viewfinder no longer freezes.** The phone's camera software sometimes frees preview pictures the app is still drawing, which froze the viewfinder until the app restarted. The app now draws only fresh frames, starts the preview afresh for each new camera stream, and holds back redraws only in short risky moments so the picture does not jump. In tests, 18 GPU errors in 61 Pro/Photo switches before, none after. ([`a87460f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/a87460fb), [`49a1bb5`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/49a1bb5d), [`f18c365`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/f18c365f), [`6d9a499`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/6d9a4997))
- **The viewfinder recovers if the GPU drops it.** After repeated GPU errors the phone can silently invalidate the viewfinder. The app now checks after every frame and rebuilds it after two failures in a row, so it was live again about 0.6 s later. ([`7e67dbf`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7e67dbf0))
- **The first Pro photo no longer crashes the app on Android 16.** The system refused to start the queue's background service. It is now started correctly, and if it is not allowed, the photo stays queued for the next app start instead of crashing every time. ([`88fd0a4`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/88fd0a49))
- **A failed RAW shot no longer blocks Pro for the whole session.** One failed RAW shot breaks RAW for every app on this phone until the camera is reopened. The app now reopens the camera itself and says "Pro shot failed. Camera restarted, try again." A second failure in a row blocks RAW until the app is restarted. ([`0e3da20`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/0e3da201), [`4a6169f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4a6169f5), [`7e4416d`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7e4416d3))
- **No more missing cameras at start-up.** Two parts of the app could write the camera's status at the same moment, so the app saw no cameras and the viewfinder stayed frozen (2 of 42 quick starts). All status changes are now made safely, and 30 of 30 quick starts opened the camera. ([`67117b7`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/67117b77))
- **Zoomed RAW shots no longer break RAW capture.** With a zoom framing, the RAW shot failed and so did later ones until the camera was reopened. Zoomed RAW shots are now taken at full sensor size and cropped afterwards, so the photo keeps its framing. ([`5e89acc`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/5e89acc3))
- **Pro shot refused while the last RAW is still being saved.** Starting a RAW shot while the previous one was still open made RAW fail until the camera was reopened. The shot is now refused with "Still saving the last photo." As a side effect, a continuous RAW burst stops after its first batch here. ([`cb6e8e1`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/cb6e8e17))
- **A shot that never finishes now fails cleanly.** If the camera drops a frame without saying so, the shutter used to stay dead until the camera reopened. The app now gives up after the expected time plus a short margin, the shutter works again, and on RAW the camera is reopened and you are told. ([`125a85c`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/125a85c6), [`be7731b`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/be7731b0))
- **Releasing the camera key during a burst no longer hangs the shot.** Letting go of the key, or clearing a tap focus, mid-burst made the camera drop the last RAW frame, so the shot hung and the camera could freeze for about 65 s. These requests now wait until the shot has ended. ([`dde2f3f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/dde2f3fd))
- **Background blur no longer comes out streaked or with black bands.** Blur passes interrupted by the viewfinder came back corrupted, and 8 MP photos got black bands. The blur now runs at normal priority and waits for the GPU after each pass: 0 of 24 test renders were damaged, against 3 of 6 before. The viewfinder may stall briefly while a photo is blurred. ([`feed375`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/feed375f), [`7e8a07f`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/7e8a07f4))
- **Magenta shadows in 3-frame RAW merges fixed.** The phone's graphics software computed one step wrongly for zero, so flat dark areas filled with red and blue spikes in 3-frame RAW merges. The step was rewritten, and the result now matches a reference calculation exactly. ([`4c39589`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4c395891))
- **Stabilisation no longer breaks the viewfinder.** Preview stabilisation is hidden on this phone, because its extra video stream made the camera close. Enhanced stabilisation is now requested only when the camera offers it, fixing a black viewfinder, and a dead preview no longer keeps stabilisation running after switching between Video and Photo. ([`4e23000`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/4e23000b), [`e3be32b`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/e3be32be), [`42df1e2`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/42df1e23))
- **Film grain data cannot be corrupted.** Two parts of the app writing the grain data at once could produce a broken file. Each now writes its own temporary file. ([`b3dd64a`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/b3dd64a3))
- **A camera without a flash never asks for flash mode.** With the flash setting on, a camera with no flash unit (for instance the front camera) was still put into flash exposure mode. That is now used only when the open camera has a flash. ([`eb686b1`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/eb686b14))

### Privacy, branding and housekeeping

- **Crash reporting to Tencent removed.** The Bugly crash reporter is gone, so no crash data or Android device ID is sent to Tencent. ([`3b01461`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/3b014615))
- **Update check turned off.** The default build used to ask upstream's server and offer to install upstream's app, which would have installed as a second separate app. It now makes no network request, downloads nothing, and the "Check for Updates" row is hidden in Settings. ([`278728b`](https://github.com/lilaccamera/lilaccamera/commit/278728b3))

## What is not listed

Changes nobody could notice are left out: debug-only switches, tests and test scripts, code tidying, build and release plumbing, and the documentation site itself. Work that was later replaced is described only as it is now. Updates that came from PhotonCamera itself are not Lilac's own work and are not listed here.
